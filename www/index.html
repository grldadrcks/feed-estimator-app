⚙️ Settings & Tools

🏭 Supplier Contact

Save your feed supplier's details. Call or email order buttons appear on the Orders tab.

🌙 Night Mode

Switch between light and dark display for comfortable day or evening use.

🔔 Delivery Reminders

Get a push notification for overdue or low-silo farms each time you open the app. Also alerts on low silo levels.

🤝 Share App

Generate a link with all your farm data. Send via text, email or WhatsApp — opens on any phone or computer.

⬇️ Download App

Save the entire app as a file. Works offline — no internet needed. Open in Chrome, Safari, or any browser.

💾 Save Data

Saves all farm data to this device. Data auto-saves every 2 minutes.

🖨️ Print Run Sheet

Print a clean delivery schedule — hides all buttons and navigation for a driver-friendly page.

📊 Export CSV

Download all farm data as a spreadsheet — open in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.

📖 How To Use

🏡 Entering Farm Data

1

Tap Farms in the bottom bar.

2

Fill in: Silo size, No. of cows, Feed rate (kg/day), Tonnes delivered, and Last delivery date.

3

Enter Feed Type (e.g. Dairy Standard, Pasture Optimiser, Milko Hi Pro) and any Extra Additives (e.g. Availa Zn, DCP, E-mag).

4

Next delivery date is predicted automatically. You can override it by typing your own date.

5

Tap ＋ Add New Farm to add more farms to the group.

📊 Reading the Dashboard

1

Top 4 cards show group totals: cows, tonnes/month, containers/week, and urgent farms.

2

Each farm shows its status badge and feed type at a glance:

On Track Due Soon Overdue

🔮 Predicted Next Delivery

  • Auto-calculated as: Last Delivery Date + Days to Empty
  • Days to Empty = Tonnes Delivered ÷ (Cows × Feed Rate ÷ 1000)
  • You can manually override the date by typing in the field.
  • Tap Reset to predicted to go back to auto-calculation.

⚠️ Reminder System

  • 🔴 Overdue / Today — Order immediately
  • 🟠 Within 3 days — Call your supplier now
  • 🟢 More than 3 days away — You're on track

📐 Formulas Used

  • Usage/Day = Cows × Feed Rate ÷ 1000
  • Days to Empty = Tonnes Delivered ÷ Usage/Day
  • Tonnes/Month = Usage/Day × 30
  • Containers/Month = Tonnes/Month ÷ 22
  • Containers/Week = Containers/Month ÷ 4.33
