📖 How To Use

🏡 Entering Farm Data

1

Tap Farms in the bottom bar.

2

Fill in: Silo size, No. of cows, Feed rate (kg/day), Tonnes delivered, and Last delivery date.

3

Enter Feed Type (e.g. Dairy Standard, Pasture Optimiser, Milko Hi Pro) and any Extra Additives (e.g. Availa Zn, DCP, E-mag).

4

Next delivery date is predicted automatically. You can override it by typing your own date.

5

Tap ＋ Add New Farm to add more farms to the group.

🔮 Predicted Next Delivery

  • Auto-calculated as: Last Delivery Date + Days to Empty
  • Days to Empty = Tonnes Delivered ÷ (Cows × Feed Rate ÷ 1000)
  • You can manually override the date by typing in the field.
  • Tap Reset to predicted to go back to auto-calculation.

⚠️ Reminder System

  • 🔴 Overdue / Today — Order immediately
  • 🟠 Within 3 days — Call your supplier now
  • 🟢 More than 3 days away — You're on track

📐 Formulas Used

  • Usage/Day = Cows × Feed Rate ÷ 1000
  • Days to Empty = Tonnes Delivered ÷ Usage/Day
  • Tonnes/Month = Usage/Day × 30
  • Containers/Month = Tonnes/Month ÷ 22
  • Containers/Week = Containers/Month ÷ 4.33
